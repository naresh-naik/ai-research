Physics studies matter, energy, motion, and forces. It seeks mathematical laws that describe the natural world.

Newton's first law states that an object remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by an external force.

Energy cannot be created or destroyed but only transformed between different forms.

Electric current flows through conductors because electrons move under the influence of an electric field.

Light behaves both as a wave and as a particle depending on the experiment.

Gravity attracts masses toward one another according to Einstein's theory of general relativity.

Sound travels through air as pressure waves.

Temperature measures the average kinetic energy of particles.

Magnetic fields arise from moving electric charges.

Quantum mechanics describes microscopic particles using probabilities instead of deterministic trajectories.
