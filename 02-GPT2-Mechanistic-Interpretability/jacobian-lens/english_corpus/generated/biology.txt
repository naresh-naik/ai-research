Biology studies living organisms and their interactions with the environment.

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of life.

DNA stores genetic information using sequences of nucleotides.

Proteins perform structural, catalytic, and signaling functions inside cells.

Photosynthesis converts sunlight into chemical energy.

Evolution explains how species change over generations through natural selection.

The nervous system transmits electrical signals throughout the body.

The immune system protects organisms against pathogens.

Enzymes accelerate biochemical reactions.

Ecosystems consist of organisms interacting with one another and their physical environment.
