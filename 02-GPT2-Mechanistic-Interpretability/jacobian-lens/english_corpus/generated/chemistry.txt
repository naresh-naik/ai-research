Chemistry studies atoms, molecules, and chemical reactions.

Atoms consist of protons, neutrons, and electrons.

Chemical bonds hold atoms together inside molecules.

The periodic table organizes elements according to atomic number.

Acids donate hydrogen ions while bases accept them.

Catalysts increase reaction rates without being consumed.

Water is a polar molecule capable of dissolving many substances.

Oxidation involves electron loss while reduction involves electron gain.

Organic chemistry focuses on carbon-containing compounds.

Chemical equilibrium occurs when forward and reverse reactions proceed at equal rates.
